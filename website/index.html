Bill Reminder — Never Miss Another Payment

Bill Reminder is a free, cross-platform application that helps you track and manage all your recurring bills and payments — subscriptions, utility bills, mobile recharges, rent, EMIs and more. It sends smart reminders by email and push notification before every due date so you never pay a late fee again.

Key Features

  • Track subscriptions, utilities, rent, EMIs and mobile recharges in one place
  • Email and push notification reminders before every due date
  • Payment history and overdue tracking
  • Secure cloud sync across all your devices
  • Offline support — works without internet
  • Household sharing — split bills with family members
  • Google sign-in — reads only your name and email, never your financial data
  • Free and open source (MIT License)

Bill Reminder never connects to your bank, never asks for card numbers, and never accesses your financial accounts. You manually enter bill names and amounts. All data is protected by row-level security — only you can see your bills.
